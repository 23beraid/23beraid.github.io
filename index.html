Aidan Berard

About

I am an undergraduate student in naval engineering at Stevens Institute of Technology. My goal is to support commerce, safety, and security in the maritime domain through engineering. Topics which interest me include seakeeping, oceanography, and hydrography. Past projects include the engineering design autonomous robot at Stevens and a concept design for a modular generator powered ship at Naval Surface Warfare Centers.

Projects

Band Stop Filter

In a circuits laboratory, my team of three people designed and implemented a band stop filter with inverting amplifier and speaker that could play sound from a computer after removing middle frequencies.

• Year: 2025

Smart Glass Windows

Myself and a teammate completed a proposed materials-enabled solution to enhance resident privacy in university residence halls using smart glass technology.

• Year: 2025

Promoting Electric Propulsion

I was a founding officer of the student club at Stevens Institute of Technology competing in the Promoting Electric Proplsion Competition, sponsored by the Office of Naval Research and the American Society of Naval Engineers. Our student team designed and raced an electric battery-powered planing vessel, winning the 2025 planing category competition.

• Year: 2025

Williamsburg Wharf

I was on the project management team for the mixed-use luxury development at Williamsburg Wharf, processing submittals, RFIs, and permits for five buildings. I completed quantity take-offs, managed prequalification for contractors, and kept track of daily construction progress.

• Year: 2024

Generator Powered Warship

Myself and a team at Naval Surface Warfare Center, Carderock Division created a concept design for a surface ship powered by modular generators and presented it to Naval Surface Warfare Center, Philadelphia Division and the Office of Naval Research.

• Year: 2024

Data Analysis of Commercial Shipping

Using data provided by the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers, myself and a teammate completed data analysis using Python to determine trends in ship operating lifespan, principal dimensions, and passenger and cargo carrying capacity for all commercial vessels in the United States.

• Year: 2024

Autonomous Robot

My engineering design team used Arduino programming to control a robot with LIDAR sensors to navigate a field and reach specific target locations. The robot was networked to external sensors to understand its position and had obstacle-avoidance and pathfinding algorithms programmed. The team created a physical design to house the controller and sensors.

• Year: 2024

Autonomous Plant Watering System

My engineering design team used Arduino programming to implement sensors measuring light and humidity and autonomously control a valve to water a plant without human intervention. The team completed a mechanical design to house the sensors, motor, valve, water reservoir, and tubing. The controller was networked to a server to record data.

• Year: 2023

11401 St. Clair Ave.

For my final architecture project, my classmates Alexa, Wyatt, and I worked with Green Movement Glenville, a nonprofit organization. Located in Cleveland's Glenville Neighborhood, the development area incudes four different parcels and a combination of both indoor and outdoor space. We created a master site plan to show how different spaces would fit together. To conclude the project, we built a piece of furniture in our school's Fab Lab. Our piece, designed for an outdoor greenhouse, innovatively combines multiple functions.

• Year: 2022

2915 Detroit Ave.

Working with a team of three other architecture students, I completed designs for a renovation of the property on Detroit for Harness Collective. This project was challenging because we were forced to consider the needs of multiple brands sharing the space. To complete this project we used Autodesk Revit to generate 3D models of our proposed design. Throughout the project we were able to work with the clients themselves to understand their visions for the space.

• Year: 2022

1811 W. 52nd St.

During my first architecture project, my partner Mason and I were tasked with renovating a historic home in Ohio City. Our floor plans detail an addition for the second floor containing a primary bedroom suite. Our goal was to consider the client's requests such as a spacious bathroom and storage space. We worked to bring new vitality and utility to the property while maintaining the ambiance and historic style. To conclude, we communicated our design by presenting to an architect.

• Year: 2022

Cerro Muleto

The Cerro Muleto hydrology project involved formulating a plan to provide clean, accessible drinking water to a remote village in Panama. Working with students from the Mastery School of Hawken, I used the Environmental Protection Agency's EPANET software to model our water distribution system. We considered source contaminants, terrain and topology, and water pressure and flow rates. In addition we created a budget for all the system components. At the end we presented our ideas to an engineer representing Engineers Without Borders.

• Year: 2022
